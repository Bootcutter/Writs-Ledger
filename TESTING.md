# Writs Ledger: in-game test checklist

1. `/reload` with Auctionator enabled: no Lua errors. Disable Auctionator: Writs Ledger does not load (required dependency).
2. `/wledger`: the window opens with ~150 rows, names coloured by tier (Journeyman green, Expert blue, Artisan purple). Escape closes it; it can be dragged. Mouse wheel and the scroll bar scroll.
3. The tier boxes are Journeyman, Expert and Artisan only (no Apprentice or Unknown). Toggle tier boxes, cycle profession, type in Search (the grey "Search" hint disappears as soon as you type and comes back when the box is empty), tick "Only what I have": the list and the "N writs shown" count follow. Click headers to sort.
3a. Click **Zones**: a panel opens below it with columns per continent (Eastern Kingdoms, Kalimdor, ..., Not learned yet). Tick only Feralas and Thousand Needles: the list shows just those writs and the button reads "Zones: 2 selected". Untick a continent box: all its zones clear. **All** restores everything ("Zones: All"); **None** empties the list. Clicking a zone's name also toggles it. Close hides the panel; closing the window hides it too.
4. Away from the AH: Buy and Scan buttons are disabled and the status line says to open the Auction House.
5. Open the AH: a red **Writs Ledger** button with a writ scroll icon sits on top of its top-right corner, in the same style and height as Auctionator's buttons. Auctionator's tabs stay at the bottom. Drag the AH window: the button stays attached. Close the AH: the button goes. If another addon already has a button at the top right of the AH, Writs Ledger's sits just to its left instead of on top of it; reopening the AH a few times puts it in the same place every time. Buy/Scan buttons enable.
6. Click **Full Scan**: Auctionator prints that the full scan started and finishes within seconds; the table fills with prices and "Last scan" says "just now" without reopening the window. Click again right away: Auctionator says how long until the next scan. A scan started from Auctionator's own tab also updates "Last scan". A plain search (Buy, or searching yourself) does **not** change "Last scan", but the searched item's price may update within 2 seconds. While a scan runs (start it from Writs Ledger or from Auctionator's own tab), every Buy button and Full Scan are greyed out, the top right reads "Auctionator scanning... N%" with N climbing, and the bottom line says Buy is paused; when it finishes the buttons come back and "Last scan" says "just now". Closing the AH mid-scan also clears the scanning text. the scan ends with "Finished processing N items" where N is not 0. On a character of the other faction, "Last scan" shows that faction's own scan time (or "never").
7. Click **Buy** under Item on Red Mageweave Pants: the Shopping tab shows only that item. **Both**: writ and item are listed together. Buy one through Auctionator.
8. Counts show from the start (Crafted Solid Shot is x200). Right-click a writ in your bags (do not accept): if its count differs, the table updates.
9. About 111 writs show a pale turn-in zone and coordinates from the start; the tooltip names the NPC. The Zones panel lists their zones from the start. Accept a writ: within a few seconds chat says "confirmed the turn-in for ...", and its Turn-in turns white. Compare with the baseline; if they differ, the confirmed one is right. Click a row: a map pin is set (or the coordinates are printed if pins are not available).
10. Turn in a writ: right away its Have column shows a green check and its name is dimmed; the row tooltip says "Already done today." Still there after /reload. On another character the check is not shown (unless the game flags it). Gone after the daily reset.
11. Carry a writ and its item: the Have column shows a gold **W** and the item count.
12. Report anything that does not fill in (prices, counts, turn-ins) together with whether the AH you use looks like the old (Classic) or new (Retail) Auction House.
13. Route fix (0.2.1): Solid Sharpening Stone, Fire Protection Potion and Nightscape Shoulders show Searing Gorge (pale) after updating. Accept one of them while in Ironforge: chat confirms Searing Gorge, not Dun Morogh. Catseye Ultra Goggles shows Feralas, Dusky Bracers Dustwallow Marsh.
14. Look (0.2.2): the window background is an even near-black, with no grey texture or inner border; the Zones panel matches.
15. Size (0.2.2): click A+ a few times: the whole window and its text grow, nothing is cut off. A- shrinks it (stops at 80%). `/wledger size 130` sets 130% and chat confirms. Options > AddOns > Writs Ledger shows the slider at the same value; dragging it resizes the open window. `/reload`: the size is kept.
16. Tooltips (0.3.0): hover a writ in your bags: below the normal lines (and Auctionator's) a green "Writs Ledger" block shows tier and reputation, "Requests N item (you have N)", the turn-in, the item cost and writ + item. Also on a writ in the AH results and on a writ linked in chat (click the link). Hover any other item: no Writs Ledger lines. A writ done today says "Done today on this character. Daily reset in ...".
17. Reset timer (0.3.0): the bottom line ends with "Daily reset in X h Y min", counting down (it updates every 2 seconds while the window is open).
18. Old prices (0.3.0): if your last scan is from yesterday or older, the top right reads "Prices are N days old: scan again." in yellow before "Last scan". After a Full Scan it goes away.
19. Shopping list (0.3.0):
    - Tick the box at the start of two rows: the "Shopping list (2)" button counts them and the list panel opens to the right of the table with a writ line and an item line for each ("0/1", "0/10"). Things already in your bags count. Untick one: its lines go.
    - Open the AH: the panel moves to the right of the AH window. Close the table: the panel stays at the AH. Close the AH with the table closed: the panel goes.
    - **Search all (N)**: Auctionator's Shopping tab lists every missing writ and item. Buy one listing: within a second its line goes up (e.g. "(5 mail) 5/10"), without opening the mailbox. Buy until a line reaches its count: green with a check. Do this for a commodity (bars, cloth) and for a writ.
    - Chat with the purchase message from a second AH addon or a chat addon: a purchase still counts once, not twice.
    - Collect the mail: the "mail" note goes, the count stays the same.
    - Click a line: a search for just that item. Right-click: the writ leaves the list. Close (X) hides the panel until you click the button again or add a writ. **Clear** empties it.
    - Turn in a listed writ: it leaves the list. Log in on another character: its list is empty (lists are per character). `/reload`: the list and the mail counts are kept.
    - While Auctionator scans, **Search all** is greyed out.
20. Report what the Auction House says in chat when you buy (copy the line), if the shopping list does not tick off.
21. Header box (0.3.1): the window title reads "Writs Ledger". Filter to one profession and tick the box at the left of the header row: every writ shown is ticked and the shopping list count goes up by that many. Click it again: they all come off. Tick one row by hand while the box is unticked, then click the box: all shown get ticked (not unticked). With a filter that shows no writs the box is greyed out.
22. Accepted writ (0.3.1): put a writ you carry on the shopping list and accept it: the line stays 1/1 (not 2/1) and its tooltip says "Accepted". Abandon the quest: within a few seconds the "Accepted" note goes.
24. Writ item tooltip (0.3.1): carry a writ and some of its requested item. Hover the item in your bags (try your bag addon and the default bags): a green "Writ item (Writs Ledger)" block names the writ, "Needs N, you have M" and "Writ in your bags". Sell or bank the writ (and take it off the shopping list): the block no longer shows on the item. Hover an ordinary item: nothing added.
